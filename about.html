A note from us

Less, but chosen with care.

Form & Field began with a simple question: what if furniture felt as good to live with as it looked? We design for the everyday—the slow Sunday morning, the extra chair pulled up, the bedroom that finally feels like a retreat.

Our collection brings together natural materials, considered proportions, and enduring shapes. Each piece is chosen to settle in, pick up a little patina, and become part of your story.
